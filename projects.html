TDT

Technology Development & Transfer

In strategic collaboration with MINT — the Ethiopian Ministry of Innovation and Technology, DAPS Analytics is architecting a comprehensive Technology Development and Transfer framework designed to accelerate Ethiopia's digital transformation agenda.

The TDT initiative bridges the gap between Ethiopia's emerging tech ecosystem and global best practices — deploying high-fidelity data infrastructure, AI capability building, and institutional knowledge transfer at national scale. DAPS Analytics serves as the lead analytics and data engineering partner, providing the intelligence layer that informs policy, optimizes resource allocation, and tracks progress across Ethiopia's technology development objectives.

TDT Delivery Framework

Phase 1 — Foundation

Data Architecture & Assessment

Comprehensive audit of existing government data systems. Designing the unified data architecture and establishing baseline infrastructure across MINT's priority sectors.

Phase 2 — Deployment

Platform Build & Integration

Deploying DAPS Analytics platforms (Onekof PM, WQIS) adapted for ministerial use. Integrating existing government databases and establishing real-time data pipelines.

Phase 3 — Transfer

Capacity Building & Handover

Training Ethiopian engineers and data scientists. Establishing domestic AI/data centers. Transferring technology ownership to MINT with ongoing DAPS advisory support.
