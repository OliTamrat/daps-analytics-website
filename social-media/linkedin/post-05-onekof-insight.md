Here is a number that surprised us when we calculated it:

**400 hours.**

That is how many hours per year a 50-person Ethiopian enterprise team loses to calendar conversion alone — manually translating every project deadline, every milestone, every payment date from the Ethiopian calendar to Gregorian and back again.

400 hours. That is 10 full work weeks. Every year. For a single company.

Now multiply that across Ethiopia's growing enterprise sector.

The root cause is straightforward: every project management tool on the market — Jira, Asana, Trello, Monday.com, Microsoft Project — was built for a 12-month Gregorian calendar. None of them natively support Ethiopia's 13-month calendar. None of them display Amharic month names. None of them handle Ethiopian Birr as a primary currency.

So Ethiopian organizations do one of two things:
① Use the tool anyway and manually convert every date (400 hours per year)
② Avoid the tool entirely and manage projects on paper or in Excel

Neither is acceptable for an enterprise operating at scale.

This is why we built Onekof PM.

Not an integration. Not a plugin. A purpose-built enterprise project management platform where the 13-month Ethiopian calendar is the default — not a workaround.

Full Amharic, Afaan Oromoo, and Tigrinya interface. Ethiopian Birr budget tracking with live exchange rate integration. AI-powered document processing for Amharic contracts. Kanban, Gantt, and analytics dashboards. Enterprise-grade security and SOC 2-aligned infrastructure.

Onekof PM is live today at onekof.com.

If your Ethiopian organization is losing weeks every year to this problem — we would like to give you those weeks back.
