We've been building quietly. Now it's time to introduce ourselves.

DAPS Analytics is a data intelligence firm engineering precision analytics, predictive AI, and high-fidelity data infrastructure for Africa and global markets.

We are not consultants who deliver reports. We build and deploy platforms that run in production — today.

Here is what is live right now:

📋 **Onekof PM** — Ethiopia's first enterprise project management platform natively built for the 13-month Ethiopian calendar, Amharic, Afaan Oromoo, Tigrinya, and Ethiopian Birr. No workarounds. No conversions. Built for Ethiopia.
→ onekof.com

💧 **WQIS** — Real-time water quality monitoring across 12 USGS stations on the Anacostia River in Washington DC. Built in partnership with the University of the District of Columbia. Live environmental intelligence for DC's watershed.

🏥 **Hakimet** — Enterprise telehealth infrastructure connecting patients, clinicians, and specialists across East Africa. Low-bandwidth optimized. Multi-language. EMR-integrated. HIPAA-aligned.
→ hakimet.com

🚛 **Olink Fleet** — AI-powered GPS fleet management for Ethiopia's commercial transport sector. Real-time tracking, predictive maintenance, driver analytics, and route optimization.

And beyond our products, we are the lead analytics partner on the TDT initiative — a national-scale technology development and transfer program with MINT, Ethiopia's Ministry of Innovation and Technology.

We founded DAPS Analytics because we kept encountering the same problem: the world's best data tools were not built for Africa's realities. Wrong calendar. Wrong language. Wrong infrastructure assumptions.

So we built the right ones.
