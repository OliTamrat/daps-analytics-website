We are proud to share that DAPS Analytics is the lead analytics and data engineering partner on one of Ethiopia's most significant technology initiatives.

The TDT — Technology Development and Transfer program — is a national-scale initiative developed in strategic collaboration with MINT, the Federal Democratic Republic of Ethiopia's Ministry of Innovation and Technology.

MINT is mandated to drive Ethiopia's digital transformation and technology adoption at a national level. The TDT framework is how that mandate becomes operational infrastructure.

Here is what DAPS Analytics is delivering as the lead data partner:

→ **National data architecture design** — building the unified data infrastructure that connects ministries, agencies, and research institutions across Ethiopia

→ **AI capability building** — deploying machine learning models and training Ethiopian institutions in predictive analytics and data-driven decision-making

→ **Knowledge transfer programs** — structured programs transferring international technology standards, engineering methodologies, and data governance frameworks

→ **Policy intelligence systems** — real-time analytics dashboards informing ministerial decision-making with speed, fidelity, and transparency

This is not a pilot. It is not a feasibility study. It is national-scale deployment.

Ethiopia has made an extraordinary commitment to building its own technology capabilities rather than simply importing solutions built elsewhere. We are honored to be part of that vision.

If you represent a government institution, international development organization, or enterprise seeking to engage on data infrastructure at scale — we would welcome a conversation.
