Africa generates enormous amounts of data every single day.

Agricultural yields across millions of smallholder farms.
Fleet movements across thousands of commercial vehicles.
Patient records across hundreds of understaffed clinics.
Water quality readings across dozens of urban watersheds.
Financial transactions across a rapidly growing mobile economy.

Most of it goes completely unused.

Not because organizations do not care about their data. Not because the technology to analyze it does not exist. But because the tools built to process, visualize, and act on that data were designed for a different world entirely — one with 12 months, one dominant language, and infrastructure assumptions that simply do not apply.

When your project management software cannot handle the Ethiopian calendar, your team spends hours every week manually converting dates.

When your telehealth platform was not designed for 2G connectivity, your rural clinicians cannot use it at all.

When your fleet management system has no context for Ethiopian road conditions, its "optimized" routes are anything but.

The tools were not built for Africa. So the data sat unused.

That is the problem DAPS Analytics was founded to solve.

Every product we build starts with a simple question: what does this environment actually need? Not what does a Western enterprise need — what does this specific context, this specific language, this specific infrastructure require?

The result is data intelligence that actually works where it is deployed.

Not adapted. Not translated. Built for it.

What data challenge is your organization currently sitting on?
