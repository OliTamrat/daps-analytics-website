Here is how a fleet manager in Addis Ababa used to start every Monday morning. 📞

7:00 AM — Call Driver 1. Where are you? Got it.
7:04 AM — Call Driver 2. Where are you? Got it.
7:08 AM — Call Driver 3...

Repeat for 40 vehicles.

By the time he finished, it was 9 AM. And the information he collected was already outdated — because every driver had moved since he called.

Then came the manual fuel logs. The paper maintenance records. The end-of-week reconciliation that took a full day because nothing connected to anything else.

And when a truck broke down unexpectedly on the Addis-Djibouti corridor — adding days to a critical delivery — there had been no warning. No signal. Nothing.

This is the operational reality for most Ethiopian commercial fleet operators. Not because they lack ambition, but because no one has built the right tools for this environment.

That changes with Olink Fleet.

**What Olink Fleet gives that manager now:**

📍 Real-time GPS location of every vehicle — on a live map, from his phone
⚡ Automatic speed and behavior alerts — no calls needed
🛣️ AI-optimized routes that account for Ethiopian road conditions and traffic patterns
🔧 Predictive maintenance alerts before breakdowns happen
⛽ Fuel consumption tracking with anomaly detection
📊 Weekly reports generated automatically every Sunday morning

Same team. Dramatically better outcomes.

This is what data intelligence looks like when it is built for the environment it operates in.
