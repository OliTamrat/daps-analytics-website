## Long Description (About section)
DAPS Analytics (Data Analytics for Practical Solutions) is a data intelligence firm engineering precision analytics, predictive models, and AI-powered platforms for organizations operating in Africa and global markets.

We are headquartered in Bethesda, Maryland with active operations in Addis Ababa, Ethiopia.

OUR LIVE PRODUCTS:

📋 Onekof PM
Ethiopia's first enterprise project management platform built natively for the 13-month Ethiopian calendar, Amharic, Afaan Oromoo, Tigrinya, and Ethiopian Birr. No workarounds. No conversions. Built for Ethiopia.
Visit: onekof.com

💧 UDC-WQIS
Unified Data Collection – Water Quality Information System. Real-time monitoring across 12 USGS stations on Washington DC's Anacostia watershed, in partnership with the University of the District of Columbia.

🏥 Hakimet
Enterprise telehealth infrastructure for East African healthcare systems. Low-bandwidth optimized, multi-language, EMR-integrated, and HIPAA-aligned.
Visit: hakimet.com

🚛 Olink Fleet
AI-powered GPS fleet management built specifically for Ethiopia's commercial transport sector. Real-time tracking, predictive maintenance, route optimization, and driver analytics.

STRATEGIC INITIATIVE:
We are the lead analytics and data engineering partner on the TDT initiative — a national-scale technology development and transfer program with MINT, Ethiopia's Ministry of Innovation and Technology.
