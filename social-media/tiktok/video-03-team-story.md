# TikTok Video 03 — Team/Founder Story

**Duration:** 45 seconds
**Format:** Talking head or photo slideshow + voiceover
**Hook Type:** Story / personal narrative

---

## FULL SCRIPT

**[0-3s] — HOOK**
Text on screen: "7 Ethiopian founders. 1 data company. Built in America."

Or spoken directly to camera:
"Let me tell you why we built DAPS Analytics."

**[3-10s] — THE PROBLEM WE LIVED**
Voiceover/talking head:
"When we were working with Ethiopian businesses and government organizations, we kept running into the same wall. Every analytics tool we tried to use had been built for a completely different world."

Text overlays:
"Wrong calendar ❌"
"Wrong language ❌"
"Wrong infrastructure ❌"

**[10-18s] — THE DECISION**
Voiceover: "So instead of adapting broken tools, we built the right ones from scratch."

Show: Quick montage of product screenshots (Onekof, WQIS, Hakimet, Olink)

Text: "4 products. Live in production. Today."

**[18-28s] — WHO WE ARE**
Voiceover: "DAPS Analytics was founded by seven specialists — from Addis Ababa to Bethesda, Maryland."

Text overlays (brief):
"Nigussie Gemechu — CEO"
"Oli Tamrat Oli — CTO"
"Fenet Jima Bedaso — Senior Researcher"
"...and 4 more"

**[28-38s] — THE MISSION**
Voiceover: "Our mission isn't complicated. Build data tools that actually work where they're deployed. Not adapted. Built for it."

Text: "Ethiopian roots. Global standards."

**[38-43s] — INVITATION**
Voiceover: "We're just getting started. Follow along."

**[43-45s] — OUTRO**
DAPS logo + "dapsanalytics.vercel.app"

## CAPTION
7 Ethiopian founders. 1 data company. Built because the world's best tools weren't built for Africa. This is DAPS Analytics. 🌍🇪🇹 #EthiopianFounders #AfricaTech #StartupStory #BlackFounders #DataAnalytics #TechTok #BuildingInAfrica #DAPSAnalytics #Ethiopia #StartupLife #AfricaRising

## PRODUCTION NOTES
- Can be recorded as talking head (founder speaking to camera) OR as voiceover over photo/video slideshow
- If talking head: good lighting, neutral background, professional but relaxed
- Tone: proud, purposeful, not salesy
- This is one of the most important videos — shows the human side of the company
