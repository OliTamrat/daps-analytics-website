## REPLY (quote or thread follow-up)
For context:

The Ethiopian calendar (Ge'ez calendar) has 12 months of 30 days each, plus a 13th month of 5 or 6 days called Pagume.

Ethiopian New Year falls in September.

The difference from Gregorian is currently 7-8 years.

Almost no software on earth accounts for this. We do.
