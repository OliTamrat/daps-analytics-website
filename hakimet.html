Healthcare in Africa deserves enterprise-grade infrastructure.

Healthcare delivery in East Africa faces a fundamental connectivity gap — clinicians are isolated from specialists, patients lack access to diagnostic resources, and administrative systems remain paper-bound. Hakimet bridges this gap with telehealth infrastructure purpose-built for African healthcare realities: low-bandwidth optimized for 2G/3G networks, natively multi-language in Amharic, Afaan Oromoo, and Tigrinya, fully EMR-integrated, and HIPAA-aligned for the clinical workflows that actually exist in Ethiopian and East African hospitals.
