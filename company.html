Welcome to DAPS

Your Partner in Data-Driven Success

In today's fast-paced business environment, your company may be sitting on a treasure trove of data — but if you're not using it strategically, you're leaving valuable opportunities on the table.

At DAPS (Data Analytics for Practical Solutions), we specialize in turning data into your competitive advantage — helping you make informed decisions that drive growth and streamline operations. Our Trajectory

Phase 01 — United States

Headquarters & Foundation

Incorporated in the United States with headquarters in Bethesda, Maryland. Delivering advanced analytics, AI solutions, and enterprise software for government and institutional clients including UDC and federal partners.

Phase 02 — Ethiopia

Operations & Product Development

Established delivery operations in Addis Ababa. Building and shipping production platforms — Onekof PM, Hakimet, and Olink Fleet — localized for Ethiopian languages, calendar, currency, and compliance.

Phase 03 — East Africa & Global

Regional & Global Scale

Expanding our platform reach across East Africa and international markets — bringing certified, production-grade data intelligence and enterprise SaaS to governments, banks, and enterprises globally.
