Welcome to DAPS

Your Partner in Data-Driven Success

In today's fast-paced business environment, your company may be sitting on a treasure trove of data — but if you're not using it strategically, you're leaving valuable opportunities on the table.

At DAPS (Data Analytics for Practical Solutions), we specialize in turning data into your competitive advantage — helping you make informed decisions that drive growth and streamline operations. Whether you're a startup looking to leverage predictive analytics or a large enterprise aiming to optimize machine learning capabilities, DAPS is your go-to partner for business intelligence and data analytics.

Based in Bethesda, Maryland, and serving clients globally, we provide tailored, actionable solutions that help businesses use their data more effectively.

Our Mission

Empowering smarter decisions through data

We believe that data analytics is not just about having the right tools — it's about using those tools in alignment with your business goals to unlock real, measurable growth. Our mission is to empower businesses with data-driven insights that guide smarter decisions and foster innovation.

We aim to be more than a service provider — we want to be an extension of your team, working alongside you to achieve success.

Our Vision

Making data accessible and actionable for all

Our vision is to make data accessible and actionable for businesses of all sizes. With our expertise in data management, governance, visualization, and advanced analytics, we help organizations understand their data and use it to create powerful impact.

Whether your business is looking to improve internal processes, optimize customer experiences, or predict future trends — we have the experience and tools to turn your data into a strategic asset.
