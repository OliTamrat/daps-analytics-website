Ethiopia's fleets deserve intelligent infrastructure.

Ethiopia's commercial transport sector operates largely on manual coordination — paper logs, phone calls, and reactive maintenance cycles. Fleet operators have no real-time visibility into vehicle location, no intelligence on route efficiency, and no early warning of mechanical failure. Olink Fleet, built by Olink Technologies (an Olink Technologies / DAPS Analytics collaboration), changes this: real-time GPS telemetry, AI-powered route optimization, driver behavior analytics, and predictive maintenance — delivering enterprise fleet intelligence to Ethiopia's transport industry.

Track, Optimize, Predict

01

Track

GPS telemetry from every vehicle in the fleet streamed in real time — live location, speed, heading, idle time, engine diagnostics, and driver identification.

02

Optimize

AI routing algorithms analyze road conditions, traffic patterns, fuel consumption data, delivery windows, and driver availability to calculate and update optimal routes dynamically.

03

Predict

Machine learning models trained on historical vehicle data detect emerging maintenance patterns and predict component failures before they cause breakdowns — reducing downtime and repair costs.

Platform Capabilities

Real-Time GPS Tracking

Live map view of the entire fleet with vehicle status, speed, location history, geofencing zones, and unauthorized movement alerts.

route

AI Route Optimization

Dynamic routing adjusted continuously for road conditions, fuel consumption, delivery windows, and driver hours — reducing fuel spend and improving on-time rates.

Driver Performance Analytics

Per-driver scoring system tracking acceleration behavior, hard braking, speed compliance, idle time, and fuel efficiency — with coaching recommendations.

Predictive Maintenance

Engine diagnostic data and sensor telemetry analyzed by ML models to predict component failures, scheduling maintenance before breakdown occurs.

Fuel Management

Consumption tracking per vehicle and route, refueling verification, anomaly detection for theft or misfueling, and cost-per-kilometer reporting.

Fleet Reporting

Automated reports on fleet utilization, on-time delivery rates, maintenance history, cost analysis, and driver performance — exportable for management review.
