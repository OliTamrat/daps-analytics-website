How We Hire

No black boxes

Hiring processes are usually opaque and slow. Ours is written down so you know exactly where you stand at every stage — and how long it should take.

1

Apply

Send your application through the role below. A CV is enough to start — a short note on why this role interests you helps a lot.

2

First response

We reply to every applicant within 5 business days, including when the answer is no. You will never be left guessing.

3

Intro conversation

A 30-minute call about your experience, what you want to build, and how DAPS actually works day to day. Ask us anything.

4

Practical session

A focused technical or domain conversation grounded in real problems we face — no whiteboard trivia, no unpaid take-home marathons.

5

Decision & offer

Final conversation with the team you would join, then a written offer with the full package explained. Typically 2–3 weeks end to end.
